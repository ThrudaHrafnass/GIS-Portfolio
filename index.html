About me

My name's Ali Purcell. I'm an aspiring geospatial scientist, with extensive experience in administrative work in a university setting.

I am currently going to school full time at Oregon State University for a BS in Geospatial Science, a minor in Fisheries & Wildlife Conservation, and a certificate in Geographic Information Systems.

I'm also the full-time administrative secretary for my local College of Science, which means I do everything from creating classes in the computer system and managing enrollment, to filing paperwork for HR and Payroll, to preparing for college events, to managing budgets. I have become a jack of all office trades.

In my free time, I write stories and go on hikes with my family.
