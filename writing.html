Writing samples

Two ArcGIS StoryMaps — examples of my science writing and science communication.

  • Effects of Land Use Change on Invasive Species Distribution
    A research proposal for a case study in the Ozark Plateau, investigating whether historical land-use change since ~1950 has facilitated the spread of invasive plants. Includes proposed remote sensing, GIS, and field sampling methods plus an annotated bibliography. (June 2026)
  • Ice, Sound, and Indigenous Populations
    A literature review on the effects of anthropogenic noise pollution on Arctic marine life and the Indigenous communities that depend on it, with embedded interactive maps and an 11-item reference list. (March 2026)
