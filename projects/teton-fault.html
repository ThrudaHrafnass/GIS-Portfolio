Teton Fault Seismic Hazard Exposure

Term project, Spatial Data Science · Oregon State University

Research question: Which developed visitor areas in Grand Teton National Park are most exposed to seismic hazard from the Teton Fault?

This project overlays the Teton Fault trace with USGS seismic hazard data and National Park Service infrastructure locations to rank the exposure of visitor centers, lodges, developed campgrounds, and major trailheads. The Teton Fault is active and capable of a magnitude 7.5 earthquake, with Holocene ruptures documented by the USGS.

Analysis in progress — maps and findings will be added when the project is complete.
